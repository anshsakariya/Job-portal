Tips for a Great Job Listing

  • Use a clear, specific job title — avoid generic titles like "Opening" or "Role".
  • Include salary range to attract 3x more qualified applicants.
  • List required skills, experience level, and growth opportunities.
  • Mention work type: Remote, Hybrid, or On-site with exact location.
  • Keep descriptions concise — ideal length is 150-300 words.
